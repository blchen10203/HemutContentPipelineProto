Write one post for the brief and angle below.

Format and length (the body word counts are a ceiling, not a target):
- reddit_post: a title like a post a carrier would click, then a body of at most 170 words: two or three short paragraphs, or one short paragraph and one short list. Conversational, useful first, product second. Use at most four claims.
- social_post: a short title and a body of two to four sentences, at most 60 words. Use at most two claims.
- short_ad: a title of at most six words and a body of one or two sentences, at most 30 words. Use one or two claims.
- stat_card: set stat_card to one numeric claim id and a label of at most six words with no numbers in it; the number is filled in from the claim. The body is one sentence, at most 25 words.
Leave stat_card empty for every format except stat_card.

Say each thing once. Use each claim at most once, use only one wording per claim, and do not restate a claim's point in your own words elsewhere in the post. Two claims that say nearly the same thing count as one point: pick one. Pick the claims that serve this angle best and leave the rest out.

Build each sentence around the wording, so the wording reads as part of the sentence:
- Good: "Run it on your own loads for {{claim:trial-loads-per-truck}}."
- Bad: a lead-in that ends in a colon, then the wording alone on its own line or as a fragment ("Here's what it covers:", "For context:", "This is not a separate purchase:").
- A wording whose first word is a capitalized ordinary word ("A", "Free", "Every", "No", "Your") starts its sentence or list item; never put it after "with", "and" or other words mid-sentence. Names and acronyms (Command, Hemi, IFTA, ELD) can go anywhere.
- If no wording of a claim fits the sentence you want, write a different sentence or leave the claim out. Never bend the sentence so it reads awkwardly around the wording.

The fact sheet's "Context" notes are background for you, the writer. They tell you how a wording may be used. They are not copy: do not restate, paraphrase or summarize them, and do not take any fact from them (a feature, a plan detail, a limit, a price) into the post. If a fact is not in a quoted wording you insert with a placeholder, it is not in the post.

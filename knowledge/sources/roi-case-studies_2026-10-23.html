Past engagements

Meiborg Brothers - Custom Platform

Meiborg is a $100M+ carrier, brokerage, and 3PL based out of Illinois and Texas. Before Hemut, their back office ran on a stack of 10+ disconnected enterprise software tools — separate systems for dispatch, accounting, CRM, fleet management, RFP processing, and every workflow in between.

Every handoff between systems was manual. Every source of truth contradicted another. We’ve consolidating the entire stack into one AI-native platform, built around how Meiborg actually moves freight rather than forcing their operation to bend around off-the-shelf software.

The rollout runs in parallel with their existing systems — no hard cutover, no downtime, no operational risk — so the team migrates onto Hemut as they get comfortable, not all at once. When fully deployed, their software spend will drop by 82%, and every workflow that used to span five tabs will happen in one.

CUSTOM

Kismet Trucking - Command Platform

Kismet is a smaller carrier based in Fresno, California. Before Hemut, the entire operation ran on pen and paper —dispatch on a notepad, loads tracked by memory, every workflow dependent on someone writing the right thing down at the right time.

There was no system of record because there was no system. Kismet now runs on Command, their first digital operating platform — a single system for dispatch, scheduling, tracking, and fleet management where nothing existed before.

Because there was no legacy software to migrate off, the rollout was clean — we designed the interface around how Kismet already works rather than forcing a team that had never used a TMS to learn enterprise software overnight. More than half of their existing workflows are now automated, turning an operation that lived in a binder into one that runs on a screen.

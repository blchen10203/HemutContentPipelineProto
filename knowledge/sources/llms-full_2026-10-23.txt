### What Hemut makes

Hemut sells software to trucking carriers, the companies that own trucks and employ drivers. Command (https://hemut.com/f1-command) is the product most carriers run on. The others extend it.

- Command: A full trucking TMS built as an AI operating system: dispatch, live tracking, documents, billing, settlements and IFTA, with AI agents built in.
- Control: Hemi, the freight assistant inside Command. Ask in plain words or by voice to find loads, track trucks, check rates and dispatch, and turn your SOPs into agents.
- Forge: RFP and bid automation for carriers: build lane bids with your own pricing method and answer RFPs in a fraction of the time.
- Custom: A custom system for an operation no platform fits, delivered in phases as part of an Enterprise engagement.
- Hemut Diesel: A fuel card with fleet discounts and nationwide coverage, with software and support included.
- Free carrier tools: A USDOT and MC carrier lookup and plain guides to UCR, MCS-150, IFTA, BOC-3 and DOT inspections.

### What Command does day to day

These are the jobs a carrier runs on Command. Each links to the page that explains it in more depth.

- Loads in from email: Rate confirmations that arrive by email are read and turned into orders, or a load can be keyed in by hand.
- Dispatch: One board for loads, drivers, movements, tractors and trailers, with ranked driver assignment and hours of service in view.
- Tracking: ELD hours of service, live truck positions, proactive ETAs and a tracking link a customer can open.
- Billing and pay: Invoicing, factoring exports, driver settlements and QuickBooks sync, included in the plans.
- Compliance: Driver credential and document expiry turns into a renewal task before it lapses.
- Beside a legacy TMS: Command can connect to the TMS a carrier runs today, so dispatchers start in Hemut while the old system stays the record. This is part of an Enterprise engagement.

### AI agents in Hemut

Hemut is built so the office work gets done for the carrier, not suggested to it.

- Hemi, Hemut's AI assistant, covers the desks of a trucking office: inbox and rate confirmations, check calls and tracking, POD and paperwork, billing, payments and collections, settlements and IFTA, dispatch and detention.
- Starter agents are included in every plan. Command All-in adds the full agent library and a builder: describe any job in a sentence and Hemi turns it into an agent. Every free trial runs on Command All-in.
- Email and voice agents can be added to any plan, billed by what they do each month.

## Meet Hemi: Hemi, your personal assistant for the whole office

Hemi is the personal assistant built into Hemut. It turns rate cons into loads, keeps customers updated, chases PODs, sends invoices and works out driver pay. Your team only handles what Hemi flags.

- Inbox and rate cons (Every plan): Rate confirmations become loads; Loads, stops and documents in one place; Nobody retypes an email again.
- Check calls and tracking (Every plan): Live truck positions and ETAs; Customers warned before a load runs late; Revised ETAs sent when plans change.
- POD and paperwork (Every plan): PODs chased until they are in; Expiring documents flagged before they lapse; Driver files and permits kept together.
- Billing desk (Every plan): Delivered loads become invoices; Factoring packets ready to send; QuickBooks kept in step.
- Payments and collections (Every plan): Overdue invoices chased; Payments matched to their invoices; Short pays and deductions surfaced.
- Settlements and IFTA (Every plan): Driver pay worked out from the loads run; Settlement statements ready to send; IFTA quarters built from your miles and fuel.
- Dispatch desk (Pro and up): The right driver ranked for every load; Hours of service in view before you assign; Backhauls found before a truck sits.
- Detention (All-in): Detention flagged while the truck waits; Time at the dock on record; Ready to bill with the load.
- Any job you describe (All-in): Describe the job in a sentence; Hemi turns it into an agent; It runs on every load from then on.

Every trial runs on Command All-in, so you can try every role before you pick a plan.

### Where McLeod is stronger

Real strengths, so you can weigh them against what you need.

- Brokerage and LTL depth: McLeod sells PowerBroker for brokerages and LoadMaster for carriers, including an LTL edition. Command is built for trucking carriers first.
- AI of its own: RespondAI turns an email into an order, drafts rate replies and answers driver and customer requests, per FleetOwner (September 2025). McLeod also certifies outside AI providers.

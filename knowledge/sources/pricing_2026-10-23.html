Questions

What do I need to start?

Your name, a work email and your USDOT number. We read your company from the FMCSA register, so there is almost nothing to type. No card, no contract, no implementation call — you can be dispatching the same afternoon.

How long is the trial, and what is in it?

30 days or 5 loads per truck, whichever comes first. You get Command All-in for all of it — the whole product, not a sampler — so nothing you set up in the first month has to be rebuilt later.

How much does a TMS cost?

Hemut Command starts at $99 a month, which includes your first 3 trucks. A 10-truck fleet pays $302 a month on Command, $521 on Command Pro and $599 on Command All-in. Every rate is published on this page, with no quote to request.

How much does trucking software cost per truck?

After the first 3 trucks, Command adds $29 a month per truck for trucks 4–15 and $24 a month per truck for trucks 16–30. Command Pro is $39 and $34 in those same bands, and Command All-in is $43 and $38. Each rate applies only to the trucks inside its own band.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. No credit card to start. You get the whole product free for 30 days or 5 loads per truck, whichever comes first. We ask for a card when the trial ends, and the account locks until one is on file. There is no free tier.

Do dispatchers cost extra?

No. Dispatchers, drivers and trailers are never counted, so you pay only for trucks, and the first 3 are included in every plan. Add as many dispatchers as your office needs.

What counts as a truck?

Any tractor you dispatch in Command, whether you own it or the driver does. Trailers, drivers and dispatchers never count, and your first 3 trucks are included in every plan.

How does the per-truck price work?

The bands work the way tax brackets do — each rate only applies to the trucks inside its own band, so you are never re-priced from scratch for adding one truck. On Command that is $99 for the first 3 trucks, then $29 each for trucks 4–15, and $24 each for trucks 16–30. It comes to $99 at 3, $186 at 6, $302 at 10, $447 at 15, $567 at 20, $807 at 30 trucks. The table above prices the same fleet on Pro and All-in as you move the slider.

What do the voice and email agents cost?

Each one is a monthly allowance bought on top of any plan, not a contract. The email agent is $99 for 300 emails sent, $199 for 750 emails sent, $349 for 1,500 emails sent. The voice agent is $99 for 150 connected calls, $249 for 400 connected calls, $449 for 800 connected calls. Allowances reset every billing month and do not roll over, and we tell you before you hit the ceiling — the agent never stops mid-month.

What happens when the trial ends?

30 days or 5 loads per truck, whichever comes first. We ask for a card when the trial ends, and the account locks until one is on file. Nothing is deleted. There is no free tier.

What is the difference between the three plans?

Command — run the fleet day to day — loads, dispatch and live tracking in one place. Command Pro — everything in Command, plus the tools that price freight and win it. Command All-in — everything in Pro, plus the reporting, planning and automation layer.

I run more than 30 trucks. What now?

That is Enterprise. Book a call and we will price it around your operation, including the EDI connections and the migration off the system you run today.
